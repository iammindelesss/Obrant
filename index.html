How it works

  • Filament = (spool price ÷ spool weight) × grams used.
  • Waste allowance = filament cost × allowance %.
  • Total production cost = filament + waste + electricity + maintenance + packaging + other.
  • Selling price = total cost × (1 + markup ÷ 100).
  • Gross profit = selling price − total cost. Margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100.

Markup is not margin. A 100% markup doubles your cost, but profit is only 50% of the selling price. The calculator shows both. Gross profit here ignores your time, tax, fees and overheads that you have not entered.

Not built yet

These are ideas for later versions and do not work today: saved print profiles, several filaments and printers, job history, downloadable quotes, and more detailed business expenses.

CAD-file analysis is also a future idea. A model’s volume does not give accurate filament use or print time, because those depend on slicer settings such as infill, walls and supports. For now, enter the figures from your slicer.

Privacy

No account, no server, no tracking. Calculations run in your browser. Your inputs are saved in this browser’s local storage so they are there next time; “Reset to defaults” clears them.
